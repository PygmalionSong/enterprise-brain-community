[中文](RELEASE_NOTES.md) · [Quick start](QUICKSTART.en.md)

Enterprise Brain helps enterprises build their own agent teams through independent storage, identity/authority, reviewed processes, controlled plans, separate business approval and durable actions/receipts. Existing ERP/SRM/WMS/CRM remain authoritative. Models are replaceable; a model proposal does not grant execution rights.

This preview includes the enterprise host/SDK, trusted MCP resource/tool contracts, structural BPMN/OData subsets, a15-domain/60-stage reference, model/analysis contracts, backup/recovery/native operations and a runnable two-process controlled first task. It excludes the complete beverage cockpit/maps and signed one-step installer, and introduces no paid offer.

Try the [first task](FIRST_TASK.en.md) and discuss a concrete problem: after a source commits but loses its response, how can a new process reconcile the original action without duplicate submission? Share a version-bound minimal report or redacted reproduction through [installation feedback](INSTALLATION_VALIDATION.en.md), [Issues](https://github.com/PygmalionSong/enterprise-brain-community/issues) or [Discussions](https://github.com/PygmalionSong/enterprise-brain-community/discussions). Never post real credentials, business data or full logs.

Apache-2.0 and third-party NOTICE are retained; maintained by PygmalionSong. The preview is not production certification and does not claim actual SAP/Dynamics integration, measured ROI, complete clean-host operations or live-model quality acceptance.


This candidate adds an English default README, Chinese/Japanese entry points, a three-language founder story, original map-free task evidence and contact guidance. No project email or paid service is launched. These are document/media changes; core, migrations, tools, tests, lockfile and workflow retain their original bytes.
